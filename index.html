Questions

What exactly do you do?

I build and manage the merch operation behind your brand: creative direction, product development, Shopify store setup, Printful integration, fulfillment configuration, and ongoing management.

How do we get started?

Send me a message about your brand, your audience, and what you have in mind. We will agree on a plan and I start with step one.

Do we need to get on a call?

No. Calls are optional. I work through messages and shared updates, and we can talk live if you would like to.

What do you need from me?

Your brand basics, links to your audience, the phrases and references that matter to you, your feedback at review, and a Shopify account to receive the transfer.

How does the ownership transfer work?

I build the store in a Shopify Partner account. When you have approved it, ownership moves to you through Shopify's transfer process. After that the store is yours, and I keep working through authorized collaborator access for the operations we have agreed on.

Who pays for what?

You do. Shopify, Printful production, shipping, payment processing, domains, and paid apps are your business's expenses. My fees are the ones in the pricing above.

How do you make sure the products are good?

Every product goes through review before the store transfers. I check print placement, legibility, colours and product details on each mockup, and nothing launches until you approve it.

What happens after the store launches?

With Store Launch, you run the store. With Managed Merch, I stay on and handle products, updates, fulfillment oversight, tracking coordination, and new collections. Growth Partner is scoped with you.

Will this make money?

I can't promise sales. I build the operation properly, and results depend on your audience and how you promote it.
