9. THE PROBLEM (PROBLEM SECTION)

Eyebrow:

The problem

Headline:

Merch is more than putting a logo on a hoodie.

Intro paragraph:

A creator can have the audience, the designs, and the products and still end up with a messy merch operation.

The store needs to work.

Products need to be set up properly. Orders need somewhere to go. Fulfillment needs to be connected. Shipping needs to make sense. Customers need to be able to track what they bought.

Those details are easy to overlook until they become someone else's problem.

Overlooked items (list):

01 — The store
A storefront that actually fits the creator's brand and makes the products easy to buy.
02 — Products
Product setup, variants, pricing, mockups, and collection organization.
03 — Fulfillment
Connecting the store to print-on-demand production and making sure the order flow works.
04 — After checkout
Shipping, tracking, order flow, and the operational side customers actually experience.
